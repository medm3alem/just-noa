# Macarena for Just Nao

`choreography/library/macarena/` is an upper-body adaptation of the classic
Macarena on the excerpt **00:48 → 01:54** of *Macarena (Bayside Boys Remix)*
by Los Del Rio (`macarena_extrait.wav`, 66.0 s, cut from the track provided
by the team).

- Timing: measured beat grid (`beats.json`): 103.15 BPM, first beat 0.235 s
  (kick drum), 7 full 16-beat cycles; every pose lands on its beat.
- Moves: right/left arm out, palms up, hands to the opposite shoulders
  (crossed), hands behind the head, on the hips, behind the hips, elbows
  pumping, "Ay!" (right fist to the sky, left arm out to the side: since
  2 Nov, for NAO's balance). Details and NAO adaptations: **docs/DANCES.md**.
- Generated by `tools/compose_choreographies.py`; checked for joint limits,
  speed (≤ 46 % of max), arm/head/body distances and balance before every
  dance.
- With the bridge v3 the music is played **by NAO**: copy the WAV to
  `/home/nao/music/macarena_extrait.wav` first (docs/AUDIO_MACARENA.md).
- **Not yet played on the physical robot**: follow
  **docs/DANCE_VALIDATION.md** (reduced speed first, operator next to NAO).
- Check that your use of the track is allowed (school demo, no distribution).
